Tools

Use these before, during, or between sessions.

Values clarification

Value discovery tool

Choose the values that matter, then narrow them to your top 5 and rank them.

Before each session

Session prep

Fill this in before your coaching session. It takes 5 minutes and makes the session sharper.

Acute stress regulation

3 minute breathing exercise

Box breathing: 4 seconds in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold. Use it when you need to regulate quickly.

Overload triage worksheet

Clarify what must be done, what can move, what should stop, and what needs a conversation.

Difficult conversation prep tool

Clarify objective, reality, likely resistance, and the one sentence that must be said clearly.

Decision journal

Capture assumptions, alternatives, risks, and why you chose one path.

7-day recovery planner

Plan recovery deliberately instead of hoping it happens on its own.

Pre-conversation grounding

Use before conflict, feedback, or a high-stakes meeting.

Post-pressure review

What happened, what cost more than it should, and what to change next time.

Weekly reflection

A different question each week. Take 10 minutes with it. The goal is not a polished answer. The goal is honesty.

Write in a notebook, a note app, or record a voice message to yourself. Come back to it before your next session.
