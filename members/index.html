Start with your situation

People do not usually need a library. They need the right thing at the right time.

I feel overloaded Burnout check, overload triage, recovery plan, boundary reset. I need clarity Decision journal, meeting filter, assumption audit, thinking tools. I need to lead a difficult conversation Conversation prep, feedback framing, debrief prompts, scripts.
