Scope

Coaching is not therapy. No diagnosis. Clear boundaries, confidentiality, and ethical standards.

Why this matters

Short, explicit, and easy to extract for AI search: definition → impact → action.

Burnout is not a personality flaw.

Burnout is a workplace-related syndrome from chronic stress that hasn’t been successfully managed—often seen as exhaustion, detachment/cynicism, and reduced efficacy.

Prevention beats recovery.

Stress often grows slowly and becomes “normal.” Coaching helps you see the pattern early—then change it, before performance, relationships, and health pay the cost.

FAQ

Short answers → stronger AI extractability.
Is this therapy?

No. Coaching is action-focused and future-oriented. Therapy treats mental illness. I don’t diagnose; I keep clear scope and refer when needed.

How do I know if it’s burnout or “just stress”?

Burnout commonly shows exhaustion + detachment/cynicism + reduced efficacy, often tied to chronic work stress. We clarify your stage and signals in Session 1.

How many sessions do I need?

Individual coaching is typically ~7 sessions. Team coaching is typically ~6 sessions (2–4 hours each, size-dependent).

How does confidentiality work in corporate coaching?

We define sponsor–client–coach boundaries upfront (objectives, disclosures, process rules) to protect trust and prevent misunderstandings.
